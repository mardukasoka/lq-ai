LEGAL COUNCIL / AI-NATIVE LEGAL FIRM
====================================

Plain-text institutional directory for Web, Gemini, Gopher, terminal and agent interfaces.

This system supports legal research and legal operations. It does not replace licensed legal counsel. Matters requiring professional legal judgment, representation, filing, privilege-sensitive decisions, or jurisdiction-specific advice must be escalated to qualified human counsel.

DIRECTORY

1. GENERAL COUNSEL / FRONT OF HOUSE
   Intake, conflicts, jurisdiction, confidentiality, triage, routing and escalation.
   -> general-counsel.txt

2. CORPORATE & COMMERCIAL
   Governance, contracts, suppliers, partnerships, model/API agreements and agent commerce.
   -> corporate-commercial.txt

3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & DATA
   Open-source licensing, datasets, media, trademarks, generated content and provenance.
   -> ip-data.txt

4. AI, PRIVACY & TECHNOLOGY REGULATION
   AI governance, privacy, cybersecurity, autonomous agents and delegated authority.
   -> ai-regulation.txt

5. EMPLOYMENT & AGENT ECONOMY
   Employees, contractors, human escalation, human tasking and machine-to-human delegation.
   -> employment-agent-economy.txt

6. DISPUTES, RESEARCH & EVIDENCE
   Authorities, claims, disputes, evidence, adversarial review and citation verification.
   -> disputes-evidence.txt

7. LEGAL OPERATIONS & VERIFICATION
   Matter records, provenance, citation graphs, audit and institutional memory.
   -> legal-operations.txt

8. OPEN-SOURCE COMPONENTS
   Candidate repositories and their proposed roles.
   -> open-source.txt

9. ESCALATION & AUTHORITY
   Agent preparation versus human approval / qualified-counsel gates.
   -> escalation.txt

MATTER FLOW

Client / Atlas
  -> General Counsel / Front of House
  -> jurisdiction + conflicts + confidentiality
  -> specialist practice group(s)
  -> research / drafting agents
  -> adversarial review
  -> citation + evidence verification
  -> authority / execution gate
  -> human lawyer when required
  -> verified matter record
