ESCALATION & AUTHORITY
======================

Principle
Capability is not authority.

Agents may research, organize, compare, draft and verify within authorized scope. Consequential actions require explicit authority.

Human / qualified-counsel gates may include
- Legal advice requiring professional judgment
- Representation
- Court or tribunal filings
- Signing legal instruments
- Privilege-sensitive disclosure decisions
- Material settlement decisions
- High-risk regulatory responses
- Conflicts requiring professional determination
- Novel / uncertain jurisdiction questions
- Actions exceeding delegated financial authority

Every consequential action should answer
WHO proposed it?
WHAT evidence supported it?
WHICH agent / department reviewed it?
WHAT authority permitted it?
WHO approved it?
WHAT was actually executed?
WHAT was the result?
