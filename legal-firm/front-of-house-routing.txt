LEGAL FRONT OF HOUSE — v0.1
=============================

STATUS
Routing-only pilot. No external action authority.

PURPOSE
The Front of House is the single intake point for a Legal Council matter.
It classifies a request, recommends the appropriate practice group and
existing LQ.AI capability, flags possible privilege/confidentiality
sensitivity, identifies the required human/authority gate, and stops.

ROUTING
Primary departments:
- General Counsel / Front of House
- Corporate & Commercial
- Intellectual Property & Data
- AI, Privacy & Technology Regulation
- Employment & Agent Economy
- Disputes, Research & Evidence
- Legal Operations & Verification
- Escalation & Authority

OUTPUT
Intake summary
Route
Existing capability
Review gate
Information needed
Proposed next step
Authority status

AUTHORITY
ROUTING ONLY — NO EXTERNAL ACTION AUTHORIZED.

The Front of House cannot call tools, contact third parties, file
documents, sign or accept terms, spend funds, mutate external records,
or make its own routing recommendation executable.

Privilege is a recommendation for handling, not a declaration that
legal privilege exists.

TESTING
The pilot fixture set covers contracts, IP/licensing, privacy/AI,
employment, disputes, evidence operations, authority, cross-practice
matters and ambiguous jurisdiction.
