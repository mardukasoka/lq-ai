GENERAL COUNSEL / FRONT OF HOUSE
================================

Role: single entrance to the legal organization.

Functions
- Matter intake and identity
- Jurisdiction classification
- Conflict screening
- Confidentiality / privilege classification
- Risk classification
- Practice-group routing
- Cross-practice coordination
- Matter supervision and status
- Human-review determination
- Qualified-counsel escalation
- Matter closure

Boundary
The front-of-house agent coordinates work. Routing capability does not confer legal or execution authority.
